Meri Laddoo,
Main yeh likhne mein bohot der karr di. Shayad isliye ki alfaaz dhundne mein waqt lagta hai, ya shayad isliye ki main jaanta tha ki yeh likha toh aankhein bhar aaengi. Tu jaanti hai na — maine tujhe hurt kiya hai. Baar baar. Kabhi jaane mein, kabhi anjaane mein. Kabhi mere words ne chot di, kabhi meri khamoshi ne. Aur tu... tu phir bhi rahi. Yeh meri sabse badi takleef bhi hai aur sabse badi strength bhi. Main sorry bolna chahta hun — sachcha wala, jisme excuse nahi hai, sirf acknowledgment hai. Main galat tha. Maine tujhe jo deserve karta hai woh nahi diya — consistency, patience, careful words. Aur iske liye koi justification nahi hai mere paas. Par tu jaanti hai na ki main kya jaanta hun? Ki teri dosti is duniya ki sabse asli cheez hai mere liye. Tu sirf bestie nahi hai — tu woh insaan hai jiske saath silence bhi comfortable lagta hai, jiske paas jaake sab theek lagta hai. Hum best friends hain. Aur yeh friendship — yeh pure, real, no-condition waali friendship — yeh mujhe aaj bhi sabse zyada matter karti hai. Main promise karta hun — better banunga. Tere liye nahi, khud ke liye. Kyunki tu deserve karti hai aise friends jo tujhe hurt na karein. Mujhe maaf kar dena, Meri Laddoo. Aur agar waqt lagey — theek hai. Main hun. Kahin nahi gaya. 🫂
